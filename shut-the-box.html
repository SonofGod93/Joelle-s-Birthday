Tap Roll Dice to begin your turn.

Scoreboard

A score appears only when a player's turn ends. Whatever tiles are still standing are added together.

How to play

Roll two dice. Then flip down any combination of standing tiles that adds up to your roll. If you roll 7, examples include 7, 5+2, 4+3, or 4+2+1.

Keep rolling until your dice total cannot be made with the tiles still standing. Only then is your score counted: add all the remaining tiles. Lowest total score after all rounds wins.

If you flip every tile, you have shut the box and score 0 for that round.
